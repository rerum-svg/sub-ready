Class binder Free early access

Set up your class once. Every sick day after that is quick.

Save your routines, seating notes, supplies and procedures for each period. Keep a no-prep emergency plan ready, and print a front-office pack any time.

Where this is saved: only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded and there’s no account. To move it to another computer, use Export and Import. The exported file holds your notes and any student first names you typed, so keep it private. Clearing your browser data deletes the binder unless you exported it.

These features are free during early access. Later they’ll be part of the Teacher plan ($5/month) and the Building plan ($49/month). Those plans aren’t on sale yet, and nothing here asks for payment.
