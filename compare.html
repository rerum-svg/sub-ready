How Sub-Ready compares

Other tools already help schools with substitute plans, and several do more than Sub-Ready. This page lists only what each company publishes on its own website, with a link and the date we checked. Prices change, so check the source before you decide.

Sub-Ready, honestly

  • Student data stays on your device. Sub-Ready is a static web page. Your plans and class binder are saved only in your browser, and student names are hidden before any AI step. There is no account and no server that stores your data.
  • It checks for gaps. It flags lines a stranger couldn’t act on (“the usual warm-up”, “the blue bin”), asks you about them, and prints “Not provided — ask the office” for anything still missing instead of making it up.
  • It costs less. The one-day tool is free. The class binder is free during early access. Planned prices are $5/month per teacher and $49/month per building. Those plans are coming soon and not on sale yet.
  • What it doesn’t do: no roster or student-information-system sync, no texting a plan to the sub, no office dashboard, no absence or substitute management. If you need those, the tools below offer them.
