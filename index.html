For teachers who are sick at 5:45 AM

Your rough notes, turned into a plan a stranger can follow.

Sub plans are hard because a substitute can’t ask you what “the usual warm-up” or “the blue bin” means. Sub-Ready finds those gaps, asks you a few quick questions, and turns your own words into a printable, minute-by-minute script.

Make a sub plan Free. No account. Nothing uploaded.

How it works

  1. Paste your shorthand. Typos, abbreviations and half-sentences are fine. Add your bell schedule and routines if you have them handy.
  2. Fill the gaps. Sub-Ready flags every line a stranger couldn’t act on and asks you 5–8 specific questions.
  3. Print the script. Times, where things are, routines, helpers, emergency info and what to leave done, in one clean page set.

What it will and won’t do

  • It only reorganizes what you wrote. It never invents activities, page numbers or rules.
  • Anything you didn’t provide shows up as Not provided — ask the office, so nothing is quietly made up.
  • Lines that use words not found in your notes are marked Check so you can review them.
  • It’s a new, small tool. Please read the final plan before you print it.

Privacy, plainly

  • Nothing is uploaded. This is a static web page. There is no server, no account, no analytics and no database.
  • Student names are hidden first. Before any AI step, the names you list are swapped for placeholders like [STUDENT_1] on your device. They’re put back only in the final printout.
  • Two ways to use AI: Chrome’s built-in on-device model (runs on your computer), or copy a ready-made prompt into any chatbot you already use. In copy mode you can see exactly what you’re pasting.
  • Don’t put passwords, medical details or student ID numbers in your notes. Keep those in your printed binder.
